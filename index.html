    Questions people ask before ordering.

    Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

    Is there a minimum order?

    It depends on the product. Tell us the item and the quantity you have in mind, and we'll confirm what's possible and the price per piece.

    What file should I send for my logo?

    A vector file (AI, EPS, PDF or SVG) prints sharpest. A high-resolution PNG or JPG also works, and we'll tell you if it needs cleaning up first.

    Will I see the design before you print?

    Yes. We show you your design on the product first, and nothing goes to print until you approve it.

    How long does an order take?

    It depends on the item and the quantity. Give us your deadline when you ask for a quote and we'll tell you whether we can meet it.

    Do you do vehicle wraps and engraving too?

    Yes. We brand cars, trucks, buses and boats with wraps and decals, and we do precision laser engraving on metal and crystal.

    Can I visit the shop?

    Yes. We're at Building 541, Ground Floor, Office 7, Old Murrah, opposite Safari Hyper Market on Salwa Road, Doha.

    Tell us what you need.

    We reply with a price per piece. Quantities, sizes and deadlines help us quote faster.
